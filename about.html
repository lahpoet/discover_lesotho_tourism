THE MOUNTAIN KINGDOM

Welcome to Lesotho

Lesotho is a landlocked country in Southern Africa, surrounded by South Africa. Its high altitude, mountain landscapes and distinct seasons give it a special character.

Maseru is the capital city. Sesotho and English are official languages, and Basotho traditions remain an important part of community life.

Visitors come for outdoor activities, scenic routes, traditional crafts and the chance to experience a welcoming culture.

Travel reminder: Check current weather, road conditions and local guidance before travelling to remote mountain areas.

A RESPONSIBLE VISIT

Travel with care

Thoughtful travel helps visitors enjoy Lesotho while respecting local people and natural places.

Greet people politely, ask permission before taking photographs, and follow local guidance at cultural sites.

Stay on suitable trails, take rubbish away with you and avoid disturbing animals or plants.

Carry warm clothing, water and a charged phone. In remote areas, arrange transport and check conditions before departure.
